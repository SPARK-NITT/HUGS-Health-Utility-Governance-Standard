# Roadmap

## Stage 0 - Seed

Foundational doctrine, terminology, and repository scaffold.

## Stage 1 - Specification

Formal controls, schemas, conformance language, examples, validator scaffolding, and research ledger.

## Stage 2 - Testable standard

Expanded validators, evidence examples, test cases, and scoring prototype.

## Stage 3 - Research-backed model

Comparative healthcare research, legal mapping, and empirical calibration.

## Stage 4 - Pilot

Voluntary or synthetic workflow pilots.

## Stage 5 - Audit framework

Repeatable assessment protocol and evidence ledger.

## Stage 6 - Policy translation

Model legislation, procurement requirements, accreditation rules, insurance regulation, institutional policy, and AI governance mappings.
