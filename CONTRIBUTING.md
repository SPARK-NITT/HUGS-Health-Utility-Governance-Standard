# Contributing

Contributions should preserve the H.U.G.S. ordering:

What is healthcare?  
What duties does healthcare create?  
Who holds authority?  
What constitutes excellence?  
How may economic systems interact with those duties?  
Who pays?

Do not convert H.U.G.S. into a financing proposal before the governance duties are defined.

Use normative language carefully:

MUST means mandatory for conformance.  
SHOULD means recommended unless justified otherwise.  
MAY means optional.

Do not claim H.U.G.S. is legally recognized, clinically validated, regulator-approved, or accreditation-certified.
