# HUGS-D07 - Non-Abandonment Doctrine

## Doctrine

Administrative boundaries, ownership transfers, insurance transitions, discharge events, or referral events may not create an unowned clinical gap when foreseeable harm can result.

## Governance meaning

This doctrine defines a conformance expectation for institutions exercising healthcare authority.

## Evidence expectation

A finding under this doctrine should connect claim, control, evidence, responsible authority, patient consequence, remediation, and verification.

## Non-goal

This doctrine does not replace clinical judgment, diagnose a patient, or claim legal recognition. It establishes governance expectations for accountable systems.
