# Severity Model

H.U.G.S. uses severity to separate ordinary improvement findings from critical failures.

advisory: useful improvement but not required for conformance.  
minor: limited nonconformance with low patient consequence.  
major: material nonconformance affecting transparency, continuity, authority, or accountability.  
critical: serious risk to patient outcome, care continuity, clinical primacy, or essential access.  
catastrophic: severe or systemic failure with substantial patient harm, abandonment, unsafe discharge, falsified records, discriminatory access, or unreviewable denial.

Critical and catastrophic failures may override aggregate scores.
