# Initial Control Catalog

## HUGS-C001 - Patient-First Classification

Doctrine: HUGS-D01

Requirement: Policies, workflows, contracts, and automated systems must not reduce medically necessary care to an ordinary consumer transaction where doing so weakens clinical duties.

Default severity: major

## HUGS-C002 - Named Clinical Owner

Doctrine: HUGS-D04

Requirement: Every active episode of significant care must identify a clinician or accountable clinical team responsible for the current care trajectory.

Default severity: critical

## HUGS-C003 - Closed-Loop Referral

Doctrine: HUGS-D04

Requirement: A referral is not complete when it is sent. It is complete when receipt is confirmed, responsibility is accepted, and material clinical information is transferred.

Default severity: major

## HUGS-C004 - Discharge Continuity Verification

Doctrine: HUGS-D04,HUGS-D07

Requirement: Discharge must include verified next-step ownership when foreseeable harm could result from a gap.

Default severity: critical

## HUGS-C005 - Financial Override Logging

Doctrine: HUGS-D03

Requirement: Any consequential financial or administrative override of a clinician’s recommendation must produce an auditable record.

Default severity: critical

## HUGS-C006 - Consequential Automation Review

Doctrine: HUGS-D08

Requirement: Automated denial, triage, discharge, authorization, or access decisions with material health consequences must provide accountable human review.

Default severity: critical

## HUGS-C007 - Material Price Transparency

Doctrine: HUGS-D06

Requirement: Patients must receive understandable material financial information before non-emergency care when reasonably possible.

Default severity: major

## HUGS-C008 - Ownership and Incentive Disclosure

Doctrine: HUGS-D06

Requirement: Material ownership, referral, compensation, or incentive relationships that could affect care pathways must be discoverable.

Default severity: major

## HUGS-C009 - Care Gap Escalation

Doctrine: HUGS-D07

Requirement: Detected care gaps must automatically enter an escalation process rather than merely producing an administrative notification.

Default severity: critical

## HUGS-C010 - Essential Access Floor

Doctrine: HUGS-D09

Requirement: Participating institutions must define and honor minimum essential-service duties independent of the profitability of individual encounters.

Default severity: critical

## HUGS-C011 - Excellence Attribution

Doctrine: HUGS-D05

Requirement: Institutional advancement systems should recognize clinical outcomes, difficult-case competence, safety, continuity, teaching, research, and peer contribution rather than rely predominantly on revenue production.

Default severity: major

## HUGS-C012 - Encounter Fragmentation Review

Doctrine: HUGS-D05

Requirement: Organizations must be capable of detecting whether operational or financial structures incentivize unnecessary fragmentation of a clinically coherent episode into multiple revenue events.

Default severity: major
