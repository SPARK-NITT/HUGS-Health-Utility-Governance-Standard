# Authority Classes

A0 through A4 are reserved for healthcare-system authority.

## A0 - Patient Authority

Rights involving consent, bodily autonomy, information, records, preferences, and participation.

## A1 - Clinical Authority

Licensed clinical judgment over diagnosis, treatment, contraindication, urgency, and medical necessity.

## A2 - Operational Authority

Scheduling, staffing, routing, logistics, facility management, and resource coordination.

## A3 - Financial Authority

Payment rules, budgeting, reimbursement, contracting, insurance, and utilization management.

## A4 - Oversight Authority

Audit, accreditation, ethics, patient safety, public regulation, and independent review.

## Precedence rule

A3 MUST NOT silently supersede A1.
