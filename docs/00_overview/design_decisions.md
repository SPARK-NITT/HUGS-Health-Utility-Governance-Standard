# Design Decisions

## H.U.G.S. is a governance standard, not a financing proposal

Financing matters, but H.U.G.S. begins earlier. It first defines healthcare duties and authority before selecting payment architecture.

## Patient status precedes consumer status

A person receiving medically necessary care is not merely purchasing an ordinary product. Clinical obligations arise from vulnerability, dependency, medical risk, professional authority, and human need.

## Excellence and extraction are separated

H.U.G.S. preserves the legitimacy of rewarding excellence while rejecting illness as an extraction opportunity.

## Continuity is a first-class object

A successful encounter is not successful healthcare if the larger care trajectory fails. The CARE_CONTINUITY_RECORD exists to make that trajectory governable.

## Critical failures override aggregate scores

No institution should hide abandonment, unsafe discharge, unreviewable consequential denial, falsified records, discriminatory access, or catastrophic care gaps behind a high composite score.
