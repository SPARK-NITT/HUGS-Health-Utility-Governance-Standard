# Purpose

H.U.G.S. creates a governance standard for reclassifying and governing medically necessary healthcare as an essential public-service function.

It applies across public, nonprofit, private, cooperative, academic, and hybrid institutions.

It asks what healthcare is before it asks who pays.

H.U.G.S. is not a clinical guideline, financing blueprint, party platform, or accreditation claim. It is a governance standard for duties, authority, continuity, economics, evidence, and accountability.
