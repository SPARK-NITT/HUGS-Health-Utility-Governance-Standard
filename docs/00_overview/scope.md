# Scope

H.U.G.S. applies to governance of medically necessary healthcare. It may be used by hospitals, physician practices, clinics, insurers, laboratories, pharmacies, rehabilitation entities, post-acute care organizations, telehealth providers, health technology providers, public agencies, and oversight bodies.

H.U.G.S. does not require one ownership model.

H.U.G.S. does not prohibit private practice.

H.U.G.S. does not treat all profit as unethical.

H.U.G.S. does not treat public ownership as automatically ethical.

The scope is governance: patient status, clinical primacy, continuity, transparent economics, excellence, public-service duty, evidence, and accountability.
